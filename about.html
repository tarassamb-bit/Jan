Company

We're building the open-source infrastructure for personal AI. Our mission is to make AI accessible, private, and owned by everyone — not just big tech companies.

Our Mission

Jan is building a future where AI is truly personal — running on your device, under your control, and connected to the models and tools you choose. We believe the future of AI isn't centralized in a few companies; it's distributed, open, and community-driven.


Every line of Jan is open source. We build in public, share our research, and invite the community to shape the product. This isn't just a business — it's a movement toward open superintelligence.

Our Values

🔒 Privacy First

Your data stays on your device. No telemetry, no tracking, no cloud dependency. Jan runs locally by default, giving you complete control over your AI experience.

🌍 Open Source

Every component of Jan is open source. Fork it, audit it, contribute to it. We believe transparency builds trust, and trust builds the future of AI.

🤝 Community Driven

Jan is built by the community, for the community. From Discord discussions to GitHub contributions, every voice matters in shaping the direction of the project.

🚀 Relentless Innovation

We push the boundaries of what's possible with local AI. Our research team develops cutting-edge models optimized for edge deployment and personal use.
